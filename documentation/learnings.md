# Learnings & Conventions

Durable learnings and project conventions captured during development.

## Conventions

### Avoid Unnecessary Variable and Parameter Abbreviations
- **Trigger:** Writing or refactoring scripts, functions, arguments, or shell variables in the codebase.
- **Learning:** Avoid cryptic or truncated abbreviations for identifiers (e.g., use `real_destination` instead of `real_dest`, `signal` instead of `sig`, `destination_file` instead of `dest_file`, `target` instead of `tgt`). Full, expressive variable names maintain clear intent, ease maintenance, and avoid ambiguity.
- **Category:** convention
- **Scope:** project-specific
- **Added:** 2026-10-09
